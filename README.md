### ◈ What We're Building

MagicSmoke is an evolving open-source initiative exploring the intersection of software and the physical world.

Our vision is to make engineering more accessible through interactive tools for designing circuits, programming robots, and experimenting with 3D simulations.

### ◈ Our Projects

- **MagicSmoke** — A browser-based robotics and simulation workspace.
- **Digital Logic Simulator** — Exploring digital logic through interactive experiments.
- **Robotics Experiments** — Learning through real-world robots, sensors, and embedded systems.

### ◈ Our Philosophy

**Design. Build. Simulate. Repeat.**

We believe the best way to understand engineering is to build things, experiment freely, and learn by doing.
